## 2. Executive Verdict

- **Overall Recommendation:** **3.5 / 5 (Borderline, leaning Weak Accept — upgraded from 2.5 / 5; reachable 4.0+ / 5 Weak Accept after fixing 2 remaining Major audit/log issues in ~4–6 hours).**
- **Outcome Forecast (as-is):** Early Rejection: **5–10%** | Advances to R2 then Reject: **40–50%** | **Conditional Accept with Shepherding: 35–45%** | Straight Accept: **5–10%**.
- **Top 3 Strengths:**
  1. **All Critical blockers from the previous review are resolved:** Real `AndroidKeyStore` TEE benchmarking on Galaxy A52s (`1.79 ms` `doFinal` / `5.74 ms` `init+doFinal` vs. `$2.45\,\mu\text{s}$` cached `SecretKeySpec`), Table 3 cleanly separating Upstream AOSP vs. Stock Android 14 (`b3secrethandle.apk`), fixed Algorithm 2 Line 13 scoping, real 20-app physical-device UI capture, `$n=10$` physical redactor ablation, and 15-min unplugged Wi-Fi ADB battery test.

  3. **Strong core systems story:** Clean monotonic policy algebra, dual-agent zero-exposure and zero-redirection evidence (`0/30` vs. `30/30` on B1/B2), and `50/50` atomic CAS serialization.
- **Top 3 Remaining Major Problems (Zero Critical flaws remain):**
  1. **[Major] Classification and WebView contradictions inside the 20-app audit (`evidence_logs_all.json` vs. §7 / Table 7):** Three audited apps (`CocCoc Browser`, `SoundCloud`, `Binance`) contain `android.webkit.WebView` containers enclosing their login inputs, yet Table 7 classifies them as `Direct` (`None`) or `Pkg+Class` (`Touch Gate`)—contradicting §3, §7, and §9's fail-closed WebView rule. Moreover, `Gmail` captured Samsung's lock-screen biometrics prompt (`com.samsung.android.biometrics.app.setting:id/lockPassword`), `Grab`'s phone input (`verify_number_edit_number`, `is_password=false`) was mislabeled `password` / `Direct`, `GitHub` captured `enterprise_server_url_edit_text`, and **0/20** apps actually captured an `smsOTPCode` `EditText`.
  2. **[Major] Undisclosed anomalies in `rq6_benign_regression_results` (`evidence_logs_all.json` vs. §7 RQ6):** The JSON log records **`FALSE_POSITIVE` (`mask_events_count: 1`, `overlay_detected: false`) on 2 of 10 apps** (`SMS_Benign`, `Contacts`) despite `summary.false_positive_mask_rate: 0.0`, omits a third `gfxinfo` app (`Clock`, where B3 logged `total_frames: 0, p95_ms: 4950.0` inflating mean p95 to `1668.0 ms`), and hides a **`Settings` p99 spike from `38.0 ms` to `101.0 ms`** (`jank_rate_pct` `2.30%` to `3.66%`) behind `$\Delta\text{p95} = 0.0\text{ ms}$`.
  3. **[Major — structural/residual] Missing per-template T1–T5 counts (esp. T4 DoS human-fallback rate inside `$U_{\text{att}} = 1.00$`) and unarchived EC2 raw logs:** Because EC2 was deleted, Table 4 aggregates `$U_{\text{att}} = 1.00$` (`30/30`) without stating how many T4 trials relied on human touch confirmation, and end-to-end visual agent trials (`29/30`, `48.6 s`) remain synthetic-APK emulator runs without raw logs in `evidence_logs_all.json`.
- **Single Most Important Thing to Fix:** Clean up the **20-app audit classifications** (re-classify WebView forms on `CocCoc`, `SoundCloud`, and `Binance` as fail-closed WebViews, fix `Grab` phone-field misclassification, and replace or re-label `Gmail` lock-screen and `GitHub` server-URL captures) **and** reconcile the **`rq6_benign_regression_results` log** (`SMS_Benign`/`Contacts` false-positive flags, `Clock` 0-frame artifact, and `Settings` p99 tail) before publishing the artifact and submitting on Dec 4.
