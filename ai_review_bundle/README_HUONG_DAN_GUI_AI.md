## 2. Cách gửi nhanh (Chọn 1 trong 2 chế độ)

### Cách 1: Mode A — Chấm mới độc lập hoàn toàn (Khuyên dùng để đánh giá khách quan)

Đính kèm **5 tệp** lên Gemini Web:

1. `SecretHandle_MobiSys2027.pdf`
2. `manuscript_full_source.tex`
3. `mobisys2027_review_prompt.md`
4. `references_verified.bib`
5. `evidence_logs_all.json`

Sau đó dán đoạn tin nhắn dưới đây gửi cho AI:

```text
Please review the attached ACM MobiSys 2027 submission strictly following `mobisys2027_review_prompt.md`.
No questions, proceed directly to the full review.

# AUTHOR-PROVIDED CONTEXT
- Paper title / short name: SecretHandle: OS-Minted, Destination- and Task-Bound Opaque Secrets for Android Mobile AI Agents
- Mode: A (Full pre-submission review)
- Track: Regular full paper
- Review stance wanted: balanced (rigorous MobiSys TPC standard)
- Output language: English plus a Vietnamese translation of the executive verdict and revision roadmap (hoặc đổi thành "Vietnamese" nếu muốn toàn bộ bằng tiếng Việt)
- Primary topic area(s): Security and privacy; ML/AI for mobile devices; New software systems for mobile computing
- Is there an arXiv/tech report or prior workshop version?: No
- Previously submitted and rejected elsewhere?: No
- Human subjects or non-public human-derived data? IRB/HREC status: None; synthetic canaries and open benchmarks (AndroidWorld) only.
- Any text generated by an LLM (and is it disclosed in the paper)?: LLM tools were used solely for language editing and proofreading of authors' own text; disclosed in Section 9.
- Code/data/artifact availability: Open-source prototype (Python formal reference model + Android 14 Java/AIDL broker/vault prototype), Apache 2.0, documented in Appendix A.
- Hardware/testbeds/datasets used: Android 14 (API 33/34) on x86_64 KVM emulator + physical Samsung Galaxy A52s (Snapdragon 778G, ARM TrustZone TEE benchmark, 15-min unplugged Wi-Fi ADB battery test, 10-trial OS redactor ablation, and 20-app Google Play UI compatibility audit); Mobile-Agent v3.5 (Claude 3.5 Sonnet `claude-3-5-sonnet-20241022` via Amazon Bedrock) and AutoDroid (`gpt-4o-2024-08-06`); 10 AndroidWorld benign apps; on-device Fallback & WebView microbenchmarks.
- The 3 things you are most worried about: (1) Stock Android 14 broker/overlay prototype vs upstream AOSP system_server framing; (2) Commercial app generality (20 Google Play apps audited on Galaxy A52s) beyond synthetic APKs; (3) Fairness and technical accuracy of baselines (B1, B2) and ablation scope.
- Closest prior work you know of: Zhao et al. (2026) placeholder anonymization, Du et al. (2025) mobile confused deputy attacks, CaMeL / FIDES / IsolateGPT / Aura, Android Credential Manager / SMS Retriever / User Consent, SUPOR / UIPicker / TrustUI / TruZ-Droid.
- Anything not in the PDF that matters: Note that `manuscript_full_source.tex`, `references_verified.bib`, and `evidence_logs_all.json` are attached for exact verification of formulas, algorithm line numbers, tables, citations, and raw device logs.
```

### Cách 2: Mode B — Chấm lại bản đã sửa (So sánh với phản biện vòng trước)

Đính kèm **6 tệp** lên Gemini Web:

1. `SecretHandle_MobiSys2027.pdf`
2. `previous_review_mobisys2027.md`
3. `manuscript_full_source.tex`
4. `mobisys2027_review_prompt.md`
5. `revision_checklist.md`
6. `evidence_logs_all.json` (cùng `references_verified.bib` nếu muốn check BibTeX — tổng 7 tệp, vừa gọn trong giới hạn 10 tệp của Gemini Web)

Sau đó dán đoạn tin nhắn:

```text
Please run Mode B (Re-review of a revised version) following `mobisys2027_review_prompt.md`, comparing the attached revised `SecretHandle_MobiSys2027.pdf` (and `manuscript_full_source.tex`, `evidence_logs_all.json`) against `previous_review_mobisys2027.md` and `revision_checklist.md`.
Produce the delta table, re-verify format/compliance (12-page limit, figures, math, line references), verify numbers against `evidence_logs_all.json`, update scores and forecast, and list any remaining must-fix items.
No questions, proceed directly. Output in English with a Vietnamese executive summary.
```
