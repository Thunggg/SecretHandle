\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Placeholder and anonymization defenses.} Mobile placeholder schemes replace sensitive rendered text with a type-preserving token and reverse the mapping at use time \cite{zhao2026anonymization,wen2023autodroid}; the closest recent work resolves a \texttt{type(PLACEHOLDER)} locally but is detector-based, session-scoped, and validates index ranges rather than which field receives a resolved value. We differ on mint source (authoritative OS source, not screen scraping), unforgeability, destination-and-task binding, typed lifecycle, and a threat model that includes redirection.

\paragraph{Capability and isolation approaches.} Planner-side capability schemes and dual-model patterns hide values from the planner in tool-calling settings \cite{debenedetti2025camel,costa2025fides}; isolation and privilege-minimization designs restrict what a tool agent may do \cite{bagdasarian2024airgap,wu2025isolategpt,shi2025progent}. These operate in the planning/tool layer rather than enforcing at the point where a secret crosses from an OS source into a UI field.

\paragraph{Prompt sanitizers.} Detection-and-rewrite approaches suppress or restore sensitive spans in prompts \cite{siyan2025papillon,zeng2025privacyrestore,chong2024casper}. They reduce model-side exposure but do not bind a resolved value to a destination and are not OS-enforced.

\paragraph{Platform and industry mechanisms.} The SMS Retriever and User Consent APIs \cite{androidSmsRetriever,androidSmsConsent}, the sensitive-notification OTP pipeline \cite{android15privacy}, Credential Manager \cite{androidCredentialManager}, autofill \cite{androidAutofill,androidAutofillServices}, the clipboard sensitivity flag \cite{androidClipboard}, and Protected Confirmation \cite{androidProtectedConfirmation} are building blocks, but autofill and Credential Manager are app-initiated and trust the app, and server-side agentic-payment tokens are payment-only. \SH{} composes these and adds OS-level destination binding and lifecycle.

\paragraph{Attacks.} Documented agent OTP exfiltration and forwarding, plus environmental and fine-print injection, motivate the threat model \cite{du2025mobileattacks,liao2024eia,chen2025fineprint,greshake2023indirect,debenedetti2024agentdojo,zhan2024injecagent}. We adapt attack mechanics, not their reported success numbers.
