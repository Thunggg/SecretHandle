\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\SH{} reframes how mobile AI agents handle sensitive credentials: by minting unforgeable, destination- and task-bound handles at authoritative OS sources and enforcing atomic zero-exposure resolution directly into verified UI fields, raw secrets never enter untrusted agent perception, cannot be redirected by indirect prompt injection, and remain immune to replay. Our empirical evaluation on Android~14 across 10 benchmark applications confirms that \SH{} eliminates secret exposure ($0/30$) and closes destination redirection ($0/30$ vs.\ $30/30$ for baselines) with negligible mediation overhead ($5.61\,\mu\text{s}$ in-vault mint, $4.2\,\text{ms}$ end-to-end) and zero benign task regression ($\Delta = 0.00$), establishing a principled foundation for OS-level secret mediation in next-generation mobile systems.
