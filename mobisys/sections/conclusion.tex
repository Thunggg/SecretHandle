\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\SH{} reframes how mobile AI agents handle sensitive credentials: instead of exposing raw secrets to visual observation and relying on fragile prompt adherence, the OS mints an unforgeable, destination-bound handle and enforces zero-exposure resolution directly into verified UI fields. Raw secrets never enter the agent's perception, cannot be redirected by prompt injection, and remain immune to replay.

Our empirical evaluation on Android~14 across 10 benchmark applications and 6 research questions confirms that \SH{} completely closes the destination redirection vulnerability ($0/30$ leakage vs.\ $30/30$ for baselines) with negligible mediation overhead ($5.61\,\mu\text{s}$ in-vault mint, $4.2\,\text{ms}$ end-to-end) and zero benign task interference. By proving privacy and autonomous agent utility coexist without mutual sacrifice, \SH{} establishes a principled foundation for OS-level secret mediation in next-generation mobile systems.
