\section{Ethics, Reproducibility, and LLM Use}
\label{sec:ethics}

\paragraph{Ethics.} Every experiment uses randomly generated synthetic canaries, an isolated loopback endpoint, and open test flows. No real credential, payment instrument, or production OTP is used or collected, and no human subjects are involved. The on-emulator reproduction runs only against a local endpoint controlled by the authors.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} Our artifact release provides two self-contained environments: (i)~an offline formal property checker (\texttt{make smoke}) that verifies state-machine invariants, policy intersection, and canary isolation across 6,400 synthetic attack runs with pinned seeds and configuration provenance; and (ii)~an on-device Android~14 evaluation harness (\texttt{device/b3-secrethandle/}) that reproduces the primary empirical results of Section~\ref{sec:evidence} with Mobile-Agent v3.5 and Claude Sonnet~4.6. All evaluation scripts, test APKs, and logging pipelines are released under the Apache~2.0 license.

\paragraph{LLM use.} Portions of the manuscript text were drafted with the assistance of a large language model; all technical claims, configuration, and numbers were reviewed and produced by the authors. This disclosure is provided for transparency.
